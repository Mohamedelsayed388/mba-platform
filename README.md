# MBA Platform

An AI-built study companion for Executive MBA courses, made by **Mohamed Elsayed**, AI Instructor.

**Live app:** https://mohamedelsayed388.github.io/mba-platform/

## Now running: Finance for Non-Financial Managers

Each lecture is published as an *issue* within 48 hours:

- **Issue page:** the lecture in English (MBA style) with the practical intuition explained in Egyptian Arabic
- **Flashcards:** terms and formulas, filterable by issue or the whole course
- **Scenario quiz:** multiple-choice cases with worked solutions and why each wrong option is wrong
- **Formula sheet:** grows with every issue

It installs on a phone like an app (Add to Home Screen) and works offline after the first visit.

> Independent study companion for the course at the Arab Academy for Management, Banking & Financial Sciences. Not an official Academy publication; the lecture slides remain the reference.
