Lecture 02 · Part One

Financial Analysis and Capital Budgeting التحليل المالي والموازنة الرأسمالية

The first CFO task in depth: where money comes from and goes, how to read statements across years and within a year, and how ratios and value metrics show whether a firm creates wealth.

What financial analysis is for الهدف من التحليل المالي

Financial analysis evaluates businesses, projects, budgets and other transactions to determine their performance and suitability. Overall, it tests whether a company earns a return on capital at least equal to its cost of capital, and generates enough cash to meet obligations and pursue opportunities.

It is used toEvaluate economic trendsSet financial policiesBuild long-term plans for business activityIdentify investment opportunities

It reads three statementsIncome statementBalance sheetCash flow statementReviewed by management, investors and lenders to assess the company's financial position.

الشركة محتاجة مؤشرين سليمين في نفس الوقت: عائد أعلى من تكلفة الفلوس، وسيولة تكفي. زي الضغط والنبض، واحد سليم مايكفيش لو التاني فيه مشكلة.

Sources and uses of funds مصادر الأموال واستخداماتها

The slide says "money (not cash)" on purpose: funds are broader than the cash in the bank. In modern finance, "Sources and Uses of Funds" is the preferred term.

CapEx · Capital Expenditure

Spending on assets that serve the business for more than one year, or that extend an existing asset's life or capacity: an MRI scanner, an operating theatre fit-out, an ambulance, a hospital IT system, a major ward renovation.

Recorded as an asset on the balance sheet and spread over its useful life as depreciation. The cash leaves at once and appears under investing activities in the cash flow statement.

OpEx · Operating Expenditure

Day-to-day costs of running the business: medicines and consumables, doctors' and nurses' salaries, electricity, routine maintenance, advertising campaigns, rent.

Recorded as an expense in the year it is incurred, so it reduces that year's profit in full.

The statement helps managers, investors and lenders see where financing comes from, how funds are allocated, whether funds are sufficient for planned activities, and whether projects are financially feasible.

Ratio analysis, ROE and the DuPont network تحليل النسب والعائد على حقوق الملكية ونظام ديبونت

Ratio analysis is a quantitative method of gaining insight into a company's liquidity, operational efficiency and profitability. It is a cornerstone of fundamental equity analysis. Two return ratios sit at the top of the network: ROE and ROA.

ROE · Return on Equity

How much profit the company earns on the shareholders' own money: share capital plus retained earnings.

ROE = Net income ÷ Shareholders' equity × 100

Shareholders' equity50,000,000

Net income this year7,500,000

ROE15%

ROA · Return on Assets

How much profit the company earns on everything it uses, whether those assets are financed by shareholders or by debt.

ROA = Net income ÷ Total assets × 100

Total assets (50M equity + 50M debt)100,000,000

Net income this year7,500,000

ROA7.5%

نفس الربح، بس الـ ROE طالع ضعف الـ ROA، والسبب إن نص الأصول متمولة بقروض. الشركة بتشتغل بفلوس البنك، والربح بيتحسب على فلوس المساهمين بس، فالعائد عليهم بيكبر. والسؤال اللي لازم يتسأل دايمًا: الـ ROE العالي ده جاي من شغل كويس، ولا من ديون كتير؟ ودي بالظبط وظيفة الـ DuPont.

The DuPont system: what is driving the ROE?

The network of ratios (also called a pyramid of ratios or the DuPont system) links ratios so that one cascades into or multiplies another, revealing the root cause of performance. DuPont splits ROE into three drivers; multiply them and sales and assets cancel out, leaving net income ÷ equity.

ROE = Net profit margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier

ROE = (Net income ÷ Sales) × (Sales ÷ Total assets) × (Total assets ÷ Equity)

DriverFormulaWhat it measures

Net profit marginNet income ÷ SalesPricing and cost control: profit kept from every 100 of sales

Asset turnoverSales ÷ Total assetsOperating efficiency: sales generated by every 1 of assets (buildings, medical equipment, inventory)

Equity multiplierTotal assets ÷ EquityFinancial leverage: how far assets are funded by debt rather than shareholders

Two private hospitalsMarginTurnoverMultiplierROE

Hospital A10%1.51.015%

Hospital B3%2.52.015%

A multiplier of 1.0 means no debt; 2.0 means half the assets are financed by debt.

المستشفيين العائد عندهم 15%، بس القصة مختلفة خالص. مستشفى A بيكسب من أسعار كويسة وتكاليف متظبطة، ومن غير ديون. مستشفى B هامشه ضعيف، وبيعوّض بعدد مرضى كبير جدًا على نفس الأسرّة والأجهزة، وبالقروض. ولو عدد المرضى قلّ أو الفايدة زادت، B هو اللي هيتعب، لأن عليه أقساط لازم تتدفع. زي الدكتور اللي مابيكتفيش بإن حرارة المريض طبيعية، وبيطلب صورة دم كاملة عشان يعرف حالته الحقيقية.

Modern techniques: MVA (Market Value Added) and EVA (Economic Value Added) الأساليب الحديثة: القيمة السوقية المضافة والقيمة الاقتصادية المضافة

MVA · Market Value Added

Total wealth a company has created for its investors since inception: current market value minus the capital originally contributed by bondholders and shareholders. Positive means wealth created; negative means wealth destroyed.

MVA = Market value (equity + debt) − Total capital invested

Worked example: Apex Corporation

Equity: 10,000,000 × $45$450M

+ Debt (market value)$150M

= Total market value$600M

− Total capital invested$400M

MVA+$200M

MetricFocusScope

MVAMarket perception and forward-looking wealth creationCumulative over the company's whole life

NOPAT · Net Operating Profit After Tax

The profit from the core business after tax, before deducting interest on loans. It measures operating performance regardless of how the company is financed.

NOPAT = EBIT × (1 − Tax rate)

EBIT (operating profit before interest and tax)20,000,000

Tax rate22.5%

NOPAT = 20M × 0.77515,500,000

EVA · Economic Value Added

The economic profit for a period after charging the full cost of all the capital used, both debt and equity, through WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital).

EVA = NOPAT − (Capital invested × WACC)

NOPAT15,500,000

Capital charge: 120M × 12% WACC14,400,000

EVA+1,100,000

ليه الـ NOPAT مابيطرحش فوايد القروض؟ لأن تكلفة القروض داخلة أصلًا جوه الـ WACC في معادلة الـ EVA، ولو طرحناها من الربح كمان، هنكون حاسبينها مرتين. وكمان عشان نقيس التشغيل نفسه: مستشفيين بنفس عدد المرضى ونفس الكفاءة، واحد متموّل بقرض والتاني بفلوس أصحابه، المفروض يطلعلهم نفس الـ NOPAT. والـ EVA الموجب هنا معناه إن الشركة كسبت 1.1 مليون فوق تكلفة كل الفلوس اللي شغالة بيها.

المستثمرين حطوا 400 مليون، والسوق شايف الشركة دلوقتي بـ 600. الـ 200 مليون الفرق هي الثروة اللي الإدارة صنعتها. أما الـ EVA فبيسأل سؤال السنة دي: الربح التشغيلي غطّى تكلفة كل الفلوس اللي شغالة في الشركة ولا لأ؟

=== KEY TERMS (flashcards) ===

• Financial analysis — التحليل المالي

Evaluating businesses, projects, budgets and transactions to judge performance and suitability. It tests whether the firm earns a return at least equal to its cost of capital and generates enough cash to meet obligations and pursue opportunities.

شرح: كشف شامل على الشركة بمؤشرين: هل بتكسب أكتر من تكلفة فلوسها؟ وهل عندها سيولة تكفي؟ لو واحد منهم مش سليم، الشركة في خطر حتى لو التاني ممتاز.

• Three financial statements — القوائم المالية الثلاث

Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement. Together they show where money came from, where it went and where it is now.

شرح: قائمة الدخل بتقولك كسبت ولا خسرت، والميزانية بتقولك عندك إيه وعليك إيه، وقائمة التدفقات بتقولك الكاش دخل وخرج منين فعلًا.

• Sources of funds — مصادر الأموال

Inflows of cash or financing: operating cash flows, bank loans, issuing shares, issuing bonds or other debt, sale of assets, retained earnings.

شرح: السؤال هنا: الفلوس جاية منين؟ من شغلنا نفسه، ولا من البنك، ولا من المساهمين، ولا بعنا أصل؟ وكل مصدر ليه تكلفة ومخاطرة مختلفة.

• Uses of funds — استخدامات الأموال

Outflows: fixed assets, working capital, debt repayment, dividends, acquisitions, R&D, CapEx, operating expenses.

شرح: والسؤال التاني: الفلوس راحت فين؟ التمويل لازم يناسب الاستخدام، وتمويل مصاريف يومية بقرض طويل الأجل علامة خطر.

• Funds vs money vs cash — الأموال والنقود والنقدية

Cash is what is available right now. Funds are broader and include credit facilities and working capital. "Sources and Uses of Funds" is the preferred term over "Sources and Uses of Money".

شرح: الكاش هو اللي في الخزنة والحساب الجاري حالًا. الـ Funds أوسع بكتير. عشان كده السلايد كاتب "money (not cash)".

• Horizontal analysis — التحليل الأفقي

Compares the same account across several years against a baseline year, often the oldest, showing which accounts grow and which shrink.

Formula: % change = (Current − Base) ÷ Base × 100

شرح: بتمشي على نفس السطر سنة ورا سنة. لو المبيعات بتكبر والمصاريف بتصغر، الاتجاه صحي. لو العكس، فيه نزيف لازم تدور على مصدره.

• Vertical analysis — التحليل الرأسي

Expresses every line as a percentage of one benchmark in the same period, most often net sales; companies then track the percentages over time.

Formula: % = Item ÷ Total sales × 100

شرح: بتاخد سنة واحدة وتسأل: من كل 100 جنيه مبيعات، كام راحوا تكلفة وكام فضلوا ربح؟

• Limits of horizontal analysis — عيوب التحليل الأفقي

Choosing a base year is difficult; it shows change without explaining the cause; it is vulnerable to changes in accounting methods; it ignores inflation.

شرح: لو المبيعات زادت 20% والتضخم 25%، إنت في الحقيقة بعت كمية أقل. الرقم كبر، بس الحجم الحقيقي صغر.

• Limits of vertical analysis — عيوب التحليل الرأسي

It does not follow accounting standards and principles, and it cannot measure the company’s liquidity.

شرح: بيوريك تركيبة القائمة كويس، لكنه مايقولكش الشركة هتقدر تدفع المرتبات والموردين الشهر الجاي ولا لأ.

• Ratio analysis — تحليل النسب المالية

A quantitative method of gaining insight into a company’s liquidity, operational efficiency and profitability; a cornerstone of fundamental equity analysis.

شرح: النسب هي التحاليل المعملية للشركة: نسبة للسيولة، ونسبة للكفاءة، ونسبة للربحية، والصورة الكاملة بتطلع لما تقراهم مع بعض.

• Network of ratios — شبكة النسب المالية

Also called a pyramid of ratios or the DuPont system. It links ratios so one cascades into or multiplies another, revealing the root cause of performance.

Formula: ROE = Net margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier

شرح: زي التشخيص التفريقي عند الدكتور: العائد نزل؟ تنزل خطوة خطوة لحد ما توصل للسبب، هامش ولا دوران ولا ديون.

• MVA (Market Value Added) — القيمة السوقية المضافة

Market Value Added. Total wealth created for investors since inception: market value of equity and debt minus the total capital they contributed. Positive creates wealth; negative destroys it.

Formula: MVA = Market value (equity + debt) − Total capital invested

شرح: المستثمرين حطوا 400 مليون، والسوق شايف الشركة بـ 600. الـ 200 مليون الفرق هي الثروة اللي الإدارة صنعتها.

• EVA (Economic Value Added) — القيمة الاقتصادية المضافة

Economic Value Added. The economic profit for a period after charging the full cost of capital, both debt and equity.

Formula: EVA = NOPAT − (Capital invested × WACC)

شرح: الشركة ممكن تكون كسبانة في الدفاتر وفي الحقيقة بتخسر، لو ربحها التشغيلي أقل من تكلفة الفلوس اللي شغالة بيها.

• MVA vs EVA — الفرق بين MVA وEVA

MVA reflects market perception and is cumulative over the company’s life. EVA reflects internal efficiency and is measured per period.

شرح: الـ EVA شهادة الأداء بتاعة السنة دي، والـ MVA رأي السوق في المشوار كله.

• Retained earnings — الأرباح المحتجزة

Cumulative profits kept in the business instead of being paid out as dividends; listed as a source of funds.

شرح: مش كاش في البنك، ده رقم بيقول الشركة احتجزت أرباح بالقيمة دي. في مثال ABC، الربح زاد 66% والمحتجز زاد 3% بس، يعني أغلب الربح اتوزع أو اتصرف.

• ROE (Return on Equity) — العائد على حقوق الملكية

The profit earned on the shareholders’ own money (share capital plus retained earnings).

Formula: ROE = Net income ÷ Shareholders’ equity × 100

شرح: أصحاب الشركة حاطين 50 مليون، والشركة كسبت 7.5 مليون، يبقى الـ ROE يساوي 15%. يعني كل 100 جنيه من فلوسهم جابت 15 جنيه. ولو شهادة البنك بتدّي أكتر من كده من غير مخاطرة، يبقى المساهم كان أحسنله يحط فلوسه في البنك.

• ROA (Return on Assets) — العائد على الأصول

The profit earned on all the assets the company uses, whether financed by shareholders or by debt.

Formula: ROA = Net income ÷ Total assets × 100

شرح: نفس الـ 7.5 مليون ربح، بس على أصول 100 مليون، نصها بقروض، يبقى الـ ROA يساوي 7.5%. والفرق بينه وبين الـ ROE بيوريك قد إيه الشركة معتمدة على الديون.

• DuPont system — نظام ديبونت

Splits ROE into three drivers: net profit margin (pricing and cost control), asset turnover (operating efficiency) and equity multiplier (financial leverage).

Formula: ROE = (Net income ÷ Sales) × (Sales ÷ Total assets) × (Total assets ÷ Equity)

شرح: شركتين العائد عندهم 15%: واحدة بتكسب من سعر وتشغيل كويس ومن غير ديون، والتانية هامشها 3% بس وبتعوّض بالتشغيل والقروض. نفس الرقم، والمخاطرة مختلفة خالص.

• NOPAT (Net Operating Profit After Tax) — صافي الربح التشغيلي بعد الضرائب

Profit from the core business after tax and before interest on loans, so it measures operations regardless of financing.

Formula: NOPAT = EBIT × (1 − Tax rate)

شرح: ربح تشغيلي 20 مليون وضريبة 22.5%، يبقى الـ NOPAT يساوي 15.5 مليون. ومابنطرحش الفوايد، لأن تكلفة القروض داخلة أصلًا في الـ WACC في معادلة الـ EVA.

• CapEx vs OpEx (Capital Expenditure vs Operating Expenditure) — المصروفات الرأسمالية مقابل التشغيلية

CapEx buys assets that serve more than a year; it is recorded on the balance sheet, depreciated over its life and shown under investing activities. OpEx covers day-to-day costs and is expensed in the year.

شرح: جهاز رنين بـ 5 مليون عمره 10 سنين: الكاش بيخرج كله مرة واحدة، لكن الربح بينزل 500 ألف إهلاك كل سنة. أما الأدوية والمرتبات وحملات الإعلان، فبيتخصموا من ربح السنة نفسها على طول.

=== FORMULAS ===

• Horizontal analysis (التحليل الأفقي): % change = (Current − Base) ÷ Base × 100. Base year is often the oldest.

• Vertical analysis (التحليل الرأسي): % = Item ÷ Total sales × 100. Net sales is the usual benchmark (100%).

• Market value added (MVA) (القيمة السوقية المضافة): MVA = Market value (equity + debt) − Total capital invested. Positive: wealth created. Negative: wealth destroyed.

• Economic value added (EVA) (القيمة الاقتصادية المضافة): EVA = NOPAT − (Capital invested × WACC). Positive: the period’s profit exceeds the full cost of capital.

• DuPont: return on equity (ROE) (نظام ديبونت للعائد على حقوق الملكية): ROE = (Net income ÷ Revenue) × (Revenue ÷ Total assets) × (Total assets ÷ Equity). Margin × turnover × leverage: shows which driver produced the ROE.

• Return on equity (ROE) (العائد على حقوق الملكية): ROE = Net income ÷ Shareholders’ equity × 100. Return on the shareholders’ own money.

• Return on assets (ROA) (العائد على الأصول): ROA = Net income ÷ Total assets × 100. Return on all assets, however financed.

• Net operating profit after tax (NOPAT) (صافي الربح التشغيلي بعد الضرائب): NOPAT = EBIT × (1 − Tax rate). Operating profit after tax, before interest; feeds EVA.

=== PRACTICE QUESTIONS WITH SOLUTIONS ===

Q1. A hospital group’s revenue was EGP 80M in the base year and EGP 92M this year. Using horizontal analysis, what is the percentage change?

- 15% ✔ correct: 12M ÷ 80M × 100. The change is divided by the base year.

- 13%: This divides by the current year (12 ÷ 92). Horizontal analysis divides by the base.

- EGP 12M: That is the absolute variance, not the percentage change.

- 87%: 80 ÷ 92 compares the two years as a ratio, not as growth.

Working:

(92 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 12 ÷ 80 × 100 = 15%

شرح: القسمة في التحليل الأفقي دايمًا على سنة الأساس. لو قسمت على السنة الحالية هتطلع بنسبة أصغر وغلط، ودي من أشهر أخطاء الامتحان.

Q2. A private clinic reports net sales of 250,000 and COGS of 175,000. In a vertical analysis, what percentage does COGS represent?

- 70% ✔ correct: 175,000 ÷ 250,000 × 100. Every line is divided by net sales.

- 30%: That is the gross profit margin, what is left after COGS.

- 143%: 250 ÷ 175 puts the benchmark in the numerator.

- 175,000: That is the amount, not its share of sales.

Working:

175,000 ÷ 250,000 × 100 = 70%

Gross profit = 100% − 70% = 30%

شرح: من كل 100 جنيه إيراد، 70 بتروح على الأدوية والمستلزمات وأجور الأطباء على الحالات، و30 بس بيتبقوا يغطّوا باقي المصاريف والربح.

Q3. Sales rose from 100,000 to 120,000 (+20%), but COGS rose from 75,000 to 97,000. What does the horizontal analysis tell management?

- Costs grew faster than sales, so gross profit fell about 8% ✔ correct: COGS +29% against sales +20%; gross profit went from 25,000 to 23,000.

- The company is healthier because sales grew 20%: Sales growth means little if costs grow faster and margin shrinks.

- Gross profit grew because both sales and COGS grew: Gross profit = sales − COGS, and it fell by 2,000.

- Nothing, horizontal analysis needs net sales as a benchmark: That describes vertical analysis.

Working:

COGS: (97,000 − 75,000) ÷ 75,000 = 29%

Gross profit: 25,000 → 23,000 = −8%

شرح: الشغل زاد لكن الربح قلّ، زي مستشفى زاد عدد المرضى بس تكلفة الخامات والأجور زادت أسرع، فالعائد من كل حالة بقى أقل.

Q4. Sales grew 20% this year, while inflation was 25%. Which weakness of horizontal analysis does this expose?

- It ignores inflation ✔ correct: Rising amounts can reflect price increases, not real growth in volume.

- It does not follow accounting standards: That is listed as a weakness of vertical analysis.

- It cannot measure liquidity: Also listed under vertical analysis, and not the issue here.

- It needs a benchmark line item: Vertical analysis uses a benchmark line; horizontal uses a base year.

Working:

Q5. 5,000,000 shares trade at $12, the market value of debt is $40M, and total capital invested by lenders and shareholders is $90M. What is the MVA?

- +$10M ✔ correct: Market value = $60M equity + $40M debt = $100M; $100M − $90M = $10M.

- −$30M: This uses equity only ($60M − $90M). The slides include debt in market value.

- +$100M: This is total market value before subtracting capital invested.

- +$50M: This subtracts debt from capital instead of adding it to market value.

Working:

Equity = 5,000,000 × $12 = $60M

Market value = $60M + $40M = $100M

MVA = $100M − $90M = +$10M

شرح: المستثمرين حطوا 90 مليون، والسوق شايف الشركة بـ 100 مليون، يبقى الإدارة صنعت 10 مليون ثروة. وافتكر إن القيمة السوقية هنا أسهم وديون مع بعض، زي مثال Apex.

Q6. A firm has NOPAT of 18M, capital invested of 150M and a WACC of 12%. What is its EVA, and what does it mean?

- EVA = 0: profit exactly covers the cost of capital, no value created ✔ correct: 150M × 12% = 18M; 18M − 18M = 0.

- EVA = +18M: the firm is creating value: This ignores the capital charge entirely.

- EVA = −132M: the firm is destroying value: This subtracts the capital itself instead of its cost.

- EVA = +15.84M: This applies 12% to NOPAT instead of to invested capital.

Working:

Capital charge = 150M × 12% = 18M

EVA = 18M − 18M = 0

شرح: الشركة كسبانة 18 مليون في الدفاتر، لكن الفلوس اللي شغالة بيها بتكلّف 18 مليون بالظبط. يعني المستثمر كان هياخد نفس العائد في بديل بنفس المخاطرة.

Q7. Which of the following is a USE of funds, not a source?

- Paying dividends to shareholders ✔ correct: Cash leaves the firm, so it is a use.

- Retained earnings: Listed as a source: profits kept inside the firm.

- Sale of assets: Listed as a source: cash comes in from the sale.

- Issuing bonds: Listed as a source: debt financing brings money in.

Working:

Sources: operations, loans, shares, bonds, asset sales, retained earnings

Uses: fixed assets, working capital, debt repayment, dividends,

acquisitions, R&D, CapEx, operating expenses

Q8. Company ABC’s net income grew 66% while retained earnings grew only 3.07%. What is the most likely explanation?

- Most of the profit was paid out as dividends or used elsewhere ✔ correct: Retained earnings rise only by the profit kept after distributions.

- The company made a loss this year: Net income grew from $15M to $25M, so there was no loss.

- Retained earnings are calculated from sales, not profit: They come from accumulated profit minus distributions.

- The two figures should always grow at the same rate: Retained earnings depend on the payout decision, so they rarely move in step.

Working:

ΔNet income = 25M − 15M = +10M (66%)

ΔRetained earnings = 67M − 65M = +2M (3.07%)

→ about 23M of this year’s 25M profit left the firm

شرح: الشركة كسبت 25 مليون السنة دي، لكن المحتجز زاد 2 مليون بس. يعني الجزء الأكبر من الربح اتوزع أو اتصرف، وده نوع الاستنتاج المطلوب من التحليل الأفقي.

Q9. Two firms both report ROE of 15%. Firm X: margin 15%, asset turnover 1.0, equity multiplier 1.0. Firm Y: margin 3%, asset turnover 2.5, equity multiplier 2.0. What does the network of ratios reveal?

- Firm Y reaches the same ROE through high turnover and heavy debt, so it carries more financial risk ✔ correct: 3% × 2.5 × 2.0 = 15%; a multiplier of 2 means half its assets are debt-financed.

- The two firms are identical because ROE is the same: The network exists to show that the same ROE can come from different causes.

- Firm X is riskier because its margin is higher: A high margin with no debt is the safer profile.

- Firm Y has the better pricing power: Its 3% margin is far weaker than X’s 15%.

Working:

X: 15% × 1.0 × 1.0 = 15%

Y: 3% × 2.5 × 2.0 = 15%

شرح: مريضين نتيجة التحليل النهائي عندهم واحدة، لكن واحد سليم طبيعي والتاني ماشي على دوا قوي. نفس الـ ROE، بس Y معتمدة على الديون، وأي مشكلة في السوق هتأثر فيها أسرع.

Q10. A hospital group has shareholders’ equity of 40M and net income of 6M this year. What is its ROE?

- 15% ✔ correct: 6M ÷ 40M × 100 = 15%.

- 6.67: This divides equity by net income (40 ÷ 6). ROE puts net income on top: 6 ÷ 40.

- 40%: This confuses the equity figure with a percentage.

- Cannot be calculated without total assets: Total assets are needed for ROA, not ROE.

Working:

ROE = 6M ÷ 40M × 100 = 15%

شرح: كل 100 جنيه من فلوس أصحاب الشركة جابت 15 جنيه ربح في السنة. وده الرقم اللي المساهم بيقارنه بأي بديل تاني، زي شهادة البنك.

Q11. Two companies each earn net income of 10M on total assets of 100M. Company X is funded entirely by equity; Company Y is funded 50M equity and 50M debt. Which statement is correct?

- Both have ROA of 10%, but Y has ROE of 20% because debt finances half its assets ✔ correct: ROA = 10M ÷ 100M for both; ROE = 10M ÷ 100M for X and 10M ÷ 50M for Y.

- Both have the same ROE because their profit is the same: ROE divides by equity, and Y has half the equity of X.

- X has the higher ROE because it has no debt: Without debt, X’s equity is larger, so its ROE is lower.

- Y has the higher ROA because it uses debt: ROA divides by total assets, which are equal, so ROA is the same.

Working:

ROA: X = Y = 10M ÷ 100M = 10%

ROE: X = 10M ÷ 100M = 10%

Y = 10M ÷ 50M = 20%

Equity multiplier: X = 1.0, Y = 2.0

شرح: الديون بتعلّي الـ ROE من غير ما الشغل نفسه يتحسن، لأن الربح بيتقسم على فلوس مساهمين أقل. وعشان كده الـ ROE العالي محتاج دايمًا تسأل: جاي منين؟

Q12. A firm reports EBIT of 30M, interest of 6M and a tax rate of 22.5%. What is its NOPAT?

- 23.25M ✔ correct: 30M × (1 − 0.225) = 23.25M. Interest is not deducted.

- 18.6M: This deducts interest first: (30M − 6M) × 0.775. That is closer to net income, not NOPAT.

- 30M: This ignores tax; NOPAT is after tax.

- 24M: This deducts interest but ignores tax.

Working:

NOPAT = EBIT × (1 − Tax rate)

= 30M × 0.775 = 23.25M

Q14. A hospital buys a new ultrasound machine for 3,000,000 in cash. It expects to use it for 6 years. How does this affect the first year?

- Cash falls by 3M now, but profit falls only by 500K of depreciation this year ✔ correct: It is CapEx: recorded as an asset and depreciated, 3M ÷ 6 = 500K a year.

- Profit falls by 3M this year: That would treat it as OpEx; an asset that serves 6 years is CapEx.

- Neither cash nor profit changes until the machine is used: The cash is paid at purchase, and depreciation starts with use in the first year.

- Cash falls by 500K a year for 6 years: Depreciation spreads the expense, not the cash; the cash was paid at once.

Working:

CapEx → asset on the balance sheet

Depreciation = 3,000,000 ÷ 6 = 500,000 a year

Cash flow: −3,000,000 in year 1 (investing activities)
